## Latency Assumptions

Gap = minimum `consumer_cycle - producer_cycle` across dst/srcs pairs within a function, without crossing loop boundaries. True latency cannot exceed this minimum.
Refuted: assumption > minimum with pairs ≥ 3. Questionable: minimum > assumption (compiler never packed it down to the assumed latency). Insufficient data: pairs < 3.
The "MCST guide" column cites Section 6.1 of the MCST guide, release 1.2, CC BY 4.0; those numbers describe **E4C/E8C, not `e2k-v6`**. Chapter 12 gives pair delays rather than per-class values; blank where the class is not in Section 6.1.
